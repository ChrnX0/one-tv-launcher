Open the help pages

Does Sofaly work on my TV?

Sofaly runs on Google TV and Android TV 10 or later: the Chromecast with Google TV, TVs with Google TV and Android TV boxes. The compatibility page lists the tested devices.

Do I need a computer to install Sofaly?

No. Install it from Google Play on the TV itself and follow the steps with the remote.

Do my apps stay on the TV?

All of them. Sofaly only replaces the home screen: Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video and the rest open as always, and the microphone button is still the Google Assistant.

Does Sofaly show ads?

No, on no plan. Sofaly sells no ads and doesn't track what you watch.

Does Sofaly come with channels or movies?

No. Sofaly organizes what's already yours: your apps, your files and open radio stations and podcasts.

How do I get Google's home screen back?

Pick Google's home screen again in the TV's settings. Nothing of yours is deleted.
