What's yours stays at home

Sofaly asks for no account, shows no ads and doesn't track what you watch. Settings, photos, messages and backups stay on the TV, or in an account of yours if you connect one. The TV only talks to the services you turn on, and the privacy policy lists every one.

Have an app? Put it on the home screen.

Sofaly already comes with ready-made cards (moon, rates, countdown, reminder). A plugin is your own Android TV app sending one more card to the home screen: the delivery order, the bus that's coming, the notice from the building. No SDK, no fee, and the app stays yours.

Quick questions

Does it work on my TV?

It works on Google TV and Android TV, like the Chromecast with Google TV, TVs with Google TV and Android TV boxes. The compatibility page lists the tested devices.

Do I need a computer to install it?

No. Install it from Google Play on the TV itself and follow the steps with the remote.

Do my apps stay?

All of them. Sofaly only replaces the home screen: Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video and the rest open as always, and the microphone button is still the Google Assistant.

Are there ads?

No, not even on the free plan. Sofaly doesn't sell ads and doesn't track what you watch.

Does it come with channels or movies?

No. Sofaly organizes what's already yours: your apps, your files and open radio stations and podcasts.

What if I want Google's home screen back?

Just pick Google's home screen again in the TV's settings. Nothing of yours is deleted.
