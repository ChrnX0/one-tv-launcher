The cross

A tidy screen anyone understands, arranged the way you like. 10 features

  • The cross

    Basic Continue, Watch, Listen, Play and Apps in one row. The world you pick grows big, with what's inside right below it.

  • Continue

    Basic One place to get back to what you were doing: the episode, the station, the podcast or the last app.

  • Top bar

    Basic Search, voice, the weather in one line and the clock, always in the same place. It folds away while you browse so the screen can breathe.

  • Google keeps working

    Basic The Google Assistant, Gemini, search and your Google account work as before. Sofaly changes the home screen, not your TV.

  • Quick panel

    Basic Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, sleep timer, sound output, alarm, profiles, notifications and the shopping list, from any app.

Watch

Your movies, your series and a home-theater player. 11 features

  • On the network

    Basic Videos, music and photos from Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, PC or NAS folders (SMB, WebDAV, FTP, SFTP, NFS), OneDrive, Dropbox, Box, Google Drive, UPnP and USB drives.

  • A player that plays everything

    Basic Dolby (AC3, E-AC3), DTS and TrueHD sound, and Blu-ray, DVD and anime subtitles, without installing Kodi or VLC.

  • Picks up where you stopped

    Basic Movies and episodes come back at the right spot. The next episode starts after 10 seconds, and after 3 in a row the TV asks if you're still watching.

  • Skip intro

    Basic When a series' intro is known, “Skip intro · OK” shows up. You can also mark the intro once and the TV remembers.

  • Preferred audio and subtitles

    Basic Original with subtitles or dubbed, chosen once and remembered per title. Subtitles from the internet when the file has none.

The house

Alarm clock, timers, automations and the connected home. 10 features

  • An alarm that turns the TV on

    Basic On time, the TV turns itself on, the volume rises slowly and there's snooze. Each alarm has a name, days, a sound and a volume, even an app or the last station.

  • Timers and hourglass

    Basic Up to 3 named timers with an on-screen notice, and a stopwatch.

  • Gentle shutdown

    Basic The sleep timer dims the screen and lowers the volume in the last 5 minutes, so nobody wakes up at the cut.

  • Turns off if nobody's watching

    Basic After the time you pick without a key press, the TV asks in large text whether you're still watching, and then sleeps.

  • Ready-made recipes

    Basic Sleep, Soap opera and Match set up the TV in one go (sound, screen and notices). One OK applies, another undoes.

  • Rain from three models

    Ultra The chance of rain is the median of three weather models (ECMWF, ICON and GFS), which misses less than any one of them.

  • Full-screen forecast

    Basic The next 24 hours, a rain chart, 9 days, air quality, the sun and the moon.

  • Rain alert

    Basic A notice over your app when rain is about to start. Time to bring the laundry in.

  • A screensaver worth leaving on

    Basic Greeting, a big clock, the date, the weather and cards with notifications, the next alarm and messages. In the Gallery theme, the painting of the day drifts slowly so nothing burns in.

Takes care of itself

Backup, maintenance and diagnosis without you remembering. 9 features

  • Locked backup, every night

    Basic While the TV is idle, a full backup locked with your password. The last 7 are kept, and every night the TV checks it would restore.

  • A copy in the cloud

    Pro Copies also go to your Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, Box, NAS or USB drive. Locked: not even the cloud can read them.

  • Overnight maintenance

    Basic Clears leftovers, repairs permissions and reschedules alarms while you sleep.

  • TV diagnosis

    Basic Internet, DNS and why an app stopped, all at once and in sentences you can understand.

  • Internet speed test

    Basic Measures your internet in one tap and shows Wi-Fi signal, latency and speed in plain words.

Privacy

No ads, no tracking, and a PIN where it matters. 5 features

  • No ads at all

    Basic Not on the free plan, not one sponsored pick. The screen is yours.

  • No tracking

    Basic Sofaly asks for no account and collects no usage data. A crash report leaves only when you send it.

  • A PIN where it matters

    Basic Anything that changes the TV asks for a PIN, and actions that delete something ask again. Too many wrong tries? The wait grows each time.
