The cross

A tidy screen anyone understands, arranged the way you like.

15

  • The cross Basic

    Continue, Watch, Listen, Play and Apps in one row. The world you pick grows big, with what's inside right below it.

    How ◀ ▶ switch worlds, ▲ ▼ go down the column, OK opens.

  • Continue Basic

    One place to get back to what you were doing: the episode, the station, the podcast or the last app.

    How It's the first item of the cross.

  • Top bar Basic

    Search, voice, the weather in one line and the clock, always in the same place. It folds away while you browse so the screen can breathe.

    How Go up to it with ▲.

  • Quick panel Basic

    Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, sleep timer, sound output, alarm, profiles, notifications and the shopping list, from any app.

    How Hold BACK.

  • Your remote, your rules Basic

    HOME always comes back to Sofaly. Holding BACK opens the quick panel, holding HOME opens the House, and holding OK on an app opens its menu.

    How A key tester shows every button the TV receives.

  • Now playing Basic

    A pill at the top with the cover and title of what's playing. OK pauses or resumes, holding OK opens the app.

    How Turn it on in Home screen › More options.

  • Themes and backgrounds Basic

    Dark, with the art of what's in focus. Gallery, with a painting from The Met every day. Sky, with satellite and map. Or a background of your own, photo or video.

    How Dark, Gallery and Sky in Basic; the others in Pro.

  • Icons you like Pro

    Use an icon pack from Play or change a single app's icon, one by one.

    How Hold OK on the app › Change icon.

  • Moon phase Pro

    Tonight's moon and how much of it is lit, worked out on the TV itself, with no internet.

    How In the forecast, on the screensaver and on the weather icon at night. Settings › Appearance.

  • Countdown Pro

    How many days are left until the date you pick, or until the next holiday.

    How In the pill at the top of the screen. Settings › Appearance.

  • House reminder Pro

    A note that comes back on the weekdays you choose: recycling day, medicine, a class.

    How In the pill at the top of the screen. Settings › Appearance.

  • Your service's status Pro

    The TV checks an address you give it and shows whether it's up: the home server, the shop's website.

    How In the pill at the top of the screen. Settings › Appearance.

  • Today's rates Pro

    Currencies, bitcoin and ether at today's value, from open official sources.

    How Next to the weather, in the top bar. Settings › Appearance.

  • Your team's next match Pro

    When and against whom your team plays next, on a card on the home screen.

    How In the TV item of the cross. Settings › Appearance.

  • Cards from other apps Pro

    Besides Sofaly's own cards, partner apps put theirs on the home screen: the delivery order, the bus, the notice from the building.

    How Cards show at the end of Watch.

Watch

Your movies, your series and a home-theater player.

10

  • On the network Basic

    Videos, music and photos from Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, PC or NAS folders (SMB, WebDAV, FTP, SFTP, NFS), OneDrive, Dropbox, Box, Google Drive, UPnP and USB drives.

    How Servers that are on and USB drives show up by themselves; the rest go in Add.

  • A player that plays everything Basic

    Dolby (AC3, E-AC3), DTS and TrueHD sound, and Blu-ray, DVD and anime subtitles, without installing Kodi or VLC.

    How Press ▼ during a video for audio, subtitles and subtitle size.

  • Picks up where you stopped Basic

    Movies and episodes come back at the right spot. The next episode starts after 10 seconds, and after 3 in a row the TV asks if you're still watching.

    How Works by itself.

  • Skip intro Basic

    When a series' intro is known, “Skip intro · OK” shows up. You can also mark the intro once and the TV remembers.

    How On out of the box.

  • Preferred audio and subtitles Basic

    Original with subtitles or dubbed, chosen once and remembered per title. Subtitles from the internet when the file has none.

    How TV and movies › Audio and subtitles.

  • See and hear better Basic

    Subtitles on a black box or in yellow, “Clearer voice” that brings out speech, and “Even volume” that tames loud movies and lifts quiet ones.

    How In the player's ▼ menu.

  • Automatic subtitles Ultra

    When there's no subtitle in your language, the TV translates the one there is.

    How Pick it in the player's subtitle menu.

  • Full movie pages Ultra

    Poster, rating, cast, trailer, age rating and the movie's collection on a beautiful page, in large text.

    How OK on a movie or series.

  • Your Trakt and Simkl lists Pro

    What you want to watch and what you've watched, synced with your account and your other devices.

    How Sign in with the QR in TV and movies › Services.

  • One search for everything Basic

    Finds movies, series, stations and apps at once, and carries the same search to YouTube, Netflix or Prime Video.

    How In the top bar, or by voice.

Play

All your games in one place, ready for the controller.

6

  • All your games in one place Basic

    GeForce NOW, Steam Link, Moonlight, PS Remote Play, RetroArch and the TV's games leave the apps row and live in Play, grouped into Cloud, From the PC, Retro and On the TV.

    How Hold OK on a game to change its group.

  • Play on the PC upstairs Basic

    The TV wakes the computer over the network, waits for it to answer and opens Moonlight or Steam Link.

    How Save the computer once in the web panel.

  • Test the network Basic

    Tells you whether your internet can handle cloud gaming, and warns before opening a cloud game on a weak network.

    How Play › Test the network.

  • Game controllers Basic

    Pair a controller without hunting for menus, see the connected ones at the top and, if you like, make a connected controller open Play.

    How Play › Pair controller.

  • Game mode Basic

    While a game is open, the screensaver, the night filter and notices wait for the match to end.

    How Turns on by itself.

  • Your Discord crew Basic

    See who from your Discord server is online and in which game, and call the crew right from the TV.

    How Turn on the server widget and paste its number.

The house

Alarm clock, timers, automations and the connected home.

10

  • An alarm that turns the TV on Basic

    On time, the TV turns itself on, the volume rises slowly and there's snooze. Each alarm has a name, days, a sound and a volume, even an app or the last station.

    How Hold BACK › Alarm.

  • Timers and hourglass Basic

    Up to 3 named timers with an on-screen notice, and a stopwatch.

    How “Hey Google, open Remind me in 10 minutes”.

  • Gentle shutdown Basic

    The sleep timer dims the screen and lowers the volume in the last 5 minutes, so nobody wakes up at the cut.

    How Timer tile in the quick panel.

  • Turns off if nobody's watching Basic

    After the time you pick without a key press, the TV asks in large text whether you're still watching, and then sleeps.

    How Screen and power › Turn off when idle.

  • Ready-made recipes Basic

    Sleep, Soap opera and Match set up the TV in one go (sound, screen and notices). One OK applies, another undoes.

    How Quick panel.

  • Automations Pro

    At a set time, when a button is pressed or when something happens (the movie paused, everyone left, rain is coming): the TV opens an app, runs a scene or turns off.

    How House and automations › Automations.

  • Home Assistant on the TV Pro

    Lights, plugs, fans, covers, air conditioning and scenes, right from the couch. It talks to your server on your network, with no cloud in between.

    How Hold HOME.

  • Who's at the door Pro

    When the doorbell rings, the camera's snapshot pops up over whatever you're watching.

    How With Home Assistant.

  • Sleeps when everyone leaves Basic

    With a UniFi router, the TV notices that the household's phones have left and goes to sleep.

    How Screen and power › More options.

  • Voice shortcuts Basic

    “Hey Google, open Weather report” reads the forecast aloud. Movies, My home, Board, timers and turning off open the same way.

    How Use the microphone button.

Weather and screensaver

The right weather and a screensaver worth leaving on.

10

  • Today's weather, measured Basic

    The temperature comes from the nearest airport's report, and the day/night icon follows sunrise and sunset where the TV is.

    How At the top of the home screen.

  • Rain from three models Ultra

    The chance of rain is the median of three weather models (ECMWF, ICON and GFS), which misses less than any one of them.

    How Shows up in the forecast by itself.

  • Full-screen forecast Basic

    The next 24 hours, a rain chart, 9 days, air quality, the sun and the moon.

    How OK on the weather.

  • Rain alert Basic

    A notice over your app when rain is about to start. Time to bring the laundry in.

    How Home screen › Weather.

  • A screensaver worth leaving on Basic

    Greeting, a big clock, the date, the weather and cards with notifications, the next alarm and messages. In the Gallery theme, the painting of the day drifts slowly so nothing burns in.

    How Screen and power › Screensaver.

  • Your photos on the screensaver Basic

    Send photos from your phone or pick a network folder. They stay in a folder only the TV can see.

    How A QR opens the panel on the right page.

  • Good morning Basic

    When you stop the alarm: greeting, time, forecast and today's agenda, and the TV can read it aloud.

    How “Hey Google, open Good morning”.

  • Google Calendar Basic

    Today's events and birthdays on the TV, from one account or all of them together.

    How OK on the date at the top.

  • Night filter Basic

    At night the screen gets warmer and softer on the eyes.

    How Screen and power › More options.

  • Weekly recap Basic

    On Sunday night, a card with screen time per app, internet used and network drops.

    How Screen and power › More options.

Takes care of itself

Backup, maintenance and diagnosis without you remembering.

8

  • Locked backup, every night Basic

    While the TV is idle, a full backup locked with your password. The last 7 are kept, and every night the TV checks it would restore.

    How System › Automatic backup.

  • A copy in the cloud Pro

    Copies also go to your Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, Box, NAS or USB drive. Locked: not even the cloud can read them.

    How System › Automatic backup.

  • Overnight maintenance Basic

    Clears leftovers, repairs permissions and reschedules alarms while you sleep.

    How Runs by itself.

  • TV diagnosis Basic

    Internet, DNS and why an app stopped, all at once and in sentences you can understand.

    How “Hey Google, open TV diagnosis”.

  • How this TV is doing Basic

    The TV measures its own numbers (time to open, crashes, alarms on time) and shows them on one screen. Nothing leaves your home.

    How System › How this TV is doing.

  • Safe mode Basic

    If a new version misbehaves right after installing, the TV falls back to a safe mode and shows the way out.

    How Shows up only when needed.

  • What changed Basic

    Each version explains what changed and brings a short checklist to try with the remote.

    How System › What changed.

  • Report a problem Basic

    Tell what happened with the remote or from the phone; the TV gathers what's needed, nothing personal.

    How System › Report a problem.

Privacy

No ads, no tracking, and a PIN where it matters.

5

  • No ads at all Basic

    Not on the free plan, not one sponsored pick. The screen is yours.

    How Always.

  • No tracking Basic

    No account, no usage analytics and no crash report leaving without you sending it.

    How Always.

  • A PIN where it matters Basic

    Anything that changes the TV asks for a PIN, and actions that delete something ask again. Too many wrong tries? The wait grows each time.

    How Remote access › PIN.

  • Private DNS Basic

    Pick a secure DNS (DNS over TLS); if it doesn't answer, the TV goes back to the previous one by itself.

    How Advanced › Network.

  • Internet per app Basic

    How much internet each app used and who it talked to, kept on the TV for 7 days.

    How Advanced › Network.
