  • The cross

    Basic

    Continue, Watch, Listen, Play and Apps in one row. The world you pick grows big, with what's inside right below it.

  • Continue

    Basic

    One place to get back to what you were doing: the episode, the station, the podcast or the last app.

  • Top bar

    Basic

    Search, voice, the weather in one line and the clock, always in the same place. It folds away while you browse so the screen can breathe.

  • Google keeps working

    Basic

    The Google Assistant, Gemini, search and your Google account work as before. Sofaly changes the home screen, not your TV.

  • Quick panel

    Basic

    Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, sleep timer, sound output, alarm, profiles, notifications and the shopping list, from any app.

  • Your remote, your rules

    Basic

    HOME always comes back to Sofaly. Holding BACK opens the quick panel, holding HOME opens the House, and holding OK on an app opens its menu.

  • Now playing

    Basic

    A pill at the top with the cover and title of what's playing. OK pauses or resumes, holding OK opens the app.

  • Themes and backgrounds

    Basic

    Dark, with the art of what's in focus. Gallery, with a painting from The Met every day. Sky, with satellite and map. Or a background of your own, photo or video.

  • Icons you like

    Pro

    Use an icon pack from Play or change a single app's icon, one by one.

  • Animations that fit your TV

    Basic

    Strong TVs animate everything; simpler ones animate only what you picked and slow down by themselves when frames drop.

  • Moon phase

    Pro

    Tonight's moon and how much of it is lit, worked out on the TV itself, with no internet.

  • Countdown

    Pro

    How many days are left until the date you pick, or until the next holiday.

  • House reminder

    Pro

    A note that comes back on the weekdays you choose: recycling day, medicine, a class.

  • Your service's status

    Pro

    The TV checks an address you give it and shows whether it's up: the home server, the shop's website.

  • Today's rates

    Pro

    Currencies, bitcoin and ether at today's value, from open official sources.

  • Your team's next match

    Pro

    When and against whom your team plays next, on a card on the home screen.

  • Cards from other apps

    Pro

    Besides Sofaly's own cards, partner apps put theirs on the home screen: the delivery order, the bus, the notice from the building.

  • On the network

    Basic

    Videos, music and photos from Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, PC or NAS folders (SMB, WebDAV, FTP, SFTP, NFS), OneDrive, Dropbox, Box, Google Drive, UPnP and USB drives.

  • A player that plays everything

    Basic

    Dolby (AC3, E-AC3), DTS and TrueHD sound, and Blu-ray, DVD and anime subtitles, without installing Kodi or VLC.

  • Picks up where you stopped

    Basic

    Movies and episodes come back at the right spot. The next episode starts after 10 seconds, and after 3 in a row the TV asks if you're still watching.

  • Skip intro

    Basic

    When a series' intro is known, “Skip intro · OK” shows up. You can also mark the intro once and the TV remembers.

  • Preferred audio and subtitles

    Basic

    Original with subtitles or dubbed, chosen once and remembered per title. Subtitles from the internet when the file has none.

  • Movies without judder

    Basic

    The TV switches to 24 Hz while a movie plays, so camera pans stay smooth.

  • See and hear better

    Basic

    Subtitles on a black box or in yellow, “Clearer voice” that brings out speech, and “Even volume” that tames loud movies and lifts quiet ones.

  • Automatic subtitles

    Ultra

    When there's no subtitle in your language, the TV translates the one there is.

  • Full movie pages

    Ultra

    Poster, rating, cast, trailer, age rating and the movie's collection on a beautiful page, in large text.

  • Your Trakt and Simkl lists

    Pro

    What you want to watch and what you've watched, synced with your account and your other devices.

  • One search for everything

    Basic

    Finds movies, series, stations and apps at once, and carries the same search to YouTube, Netflix or Prime Video.

  • An alarm that turns the TV on

    Basic

    On time, the TV turns itself on, the volume rises slowly and there's snooze. Each alarm has a name, days, a sound and a volume, even an app or the last station.

  • Timers and hourglass

    Basic

    Up to 3 named timers with an on-screen notice, and a stopwatch.

  • Gentle shutdown

    Basic

    The sleep timer dims the screen and lowers the volume in the last 5 minutes, so nobody wakes up at the cut.

  • Turns off if nobody's watching

    Basic

    After the time you pick without a key press, the TV asks in large text whether you're still watching, and then sleeps.

  • Ready-made recipes

    Basic

    Sleep, Soap opera and Match set up the TV in one go (sound, screen and notices). One OK applies, another undoes.

  • Automations

    Pro

    At a set time, when a button is pressed or when something happens (the movie paused, everyone left, rain is coming): the TV opens an app, runs a scene or turns off.

  • Home Assistant on the TV

    Pro

    Lights, plugs, fans, covers, air conditioning and scenes, right from the couch. It talks to your server on your network, with no cloud in between.

  • Who's at the door

    Pro

    When the doorbell rings, the camera's snapshot pops up over whatever you're watching.

  • Sleeps when everyone leaves

    Basic

    With a UniFi router, the TV notices that the household's phones have left and goes to sleep.

  • Voice shortcuts

    Basic

    “Hey Google, open Weather report” reads the forecast aloud. Movies, My home, Board, timers and turning off open the same way.

Takes care of itself

Backup, maintenance and diagnosis without you remembering. 9 features

Takes care of itself
  • Locked backup, every night
  • A copy in the cloud
  • Overnight maintenance
  • TV diagnosis
  • Internet speed test

  • Locked backup, every night

    Basic

    While the TV is idle, a full backup locked with your password. The last 7 are kept, and every night the TV checks it would restore.

  • A copy in the cloud

    Pro

    Copies also go to your Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, Box, NAS or USB drive. Locked: not even the cloud can read them.

  • Overnight maintenance

    Basic

    Clears leftovers, repairs permissions and reschedules alarms while you sleep.

  • TV diagnosis

    Basic

    Internet, DNS and why an app stopped, all at once and in sentences you can understand.

  • Internet speed test

    Basic

    Measures your internet in one tap and shows Wi-Fi signal, latency and speed in plain words.

  • How this TV is doing

    Basic

    The TV measures its own numbers (time to open, crashes, alarms on time) and shows them on one screen. Nothing leaves your home.

  • Safe mode

    Basic

    If a new version misbehaves right after installing, the TV falls back to a safe mode and shows the way out.

  • What changed

    Basic

    Each version explains what changed and brings a short checklist to try with the remote.

  • Report a problem

    Basic

    Tell what happened with the remote or from the phone; the TV gathers what's needed, nothing personal.

Privacy

No ads, no tracking, and a PIN where it matters. 5 features

Privacy
  • No ads at all
  • No tracking
  • A PIN where it matters
  • Private DNS
  • Internet per app

  • No ads at all

    Basic

    Not on the free plan, not one sponsored pick. The screen is yours.

  • No tracking

    Basic

    No account, no usage analytics and no crash report leaving without you sending it.

  • A PIN where it matters

    Basic

    Anything that changes the TV asks for a PIN, and actions that delete something ask again. Too many wrong tries? The wait grows each time.

  • Private DNS

    Basic

    Pick a secure DNS (DNS over TLS); if it doesn't answer, the TV goes back to the previous one by itself.

  • Internet per app

    Basic

    How much internet each app used and who it talked to, kept on the TV for 7 days.
