Does it work on your TV?

Every device below runs the same four checks. The numbers are measured by the launcher itself on that device, not typed by us.

  • Home button: pressing Home always lands on One TV.
  • Fast wake: from screen on to the home screen in under 2 seconds.
  • 24 h stable: no crash in 24 hours of use.
  • Screens: no cut or overlapping text (checked on a computer for each release).

How to measure your own device

Install the launcher and use it for a day. Then open the web panel and go to /api/extras/ideias/compatibilidade, or run:

adb shell am broadcast -n br.paulo.tvlauncher/.ControleShizukuAdbReceiver -a br.paulo.tvlauncher.EXTRAS --es o compat

The report holds only the model, the system version and the measurements: no serial number, account, network or name.
