Your app on the home screen people actually look at.

A Sofaly plugin is your own Android TV app. It sends a short card and Sofaly draws it on the home screen, in large text and in the house style.

Why build a plugin

  • Up front, not in a drawer

    Your card sits on the home screen, where the family looks every time the TV turns on, not lost in a list of apps.

  • Plain Android code

    One ContentProvider and one row of data, written with the Android you already know. Building and showing a card costs nothing.

  • Your app, your business

    Publish on Play however you like, free or paid. Sofaly only draws the card; the app and the customers are yours.

Two steps

1. Declare a provider with Sofaly's action

<provider
    android:name=".CardProvider"
    android:authorities="com.example.score.card"
    android:exported="true">
    <intent-filter>
        <action android:name="tv.sofaly.plugin.CARTAO" />
    </intent-filter>
</provider>

2. Answer with one row: title, value, detail and color

override fun query(uri: Uri, p: Array<String>?, s: String?,
                   a: Array<String>?, o: String?): Cursor =
    MatrixCursor(arrayOf("titulo", "valor", "detalhe", "cor")).apply {
        addRow(arrayOf("Match score", "2 x 1",
                       "38' second half", "#1E7A46"))
    }

Rules that protect the viewer

  • You never draw on the home screen: you send text and a color, Sofaly draws.
  • Sofaly never sends you anything about the person's apps, accounts or what they watch.
  • Plugins are part of the Pro plan, and the person chooses which ones show.
